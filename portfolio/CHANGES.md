## Round 3 — reframed as a business profile

The client asked to drop Madras High Court and present the document as a **business profile**
rather than an advocate's portfolio. The hierarchy was inverted:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Name sat under "Madras High Court · Tamil Nadu, India" | Name sits under **"Business Owner"**, then "Innoaccess FZE — Garments ∙ Pharma ∙ Cars ∙ Logistics" |
| Header credentials: In Practice / Enrolled / Panel Advocate / Based In | **Based In** Dubai, U.A.E. · **Presence** U.A.E. · India · Malaysia · **Legal Practice** 22 Years |
| Profile opened with legal practice | Opens with the businesses; legal practice follows as a supporting line |
| Business sat in the right column, below Practice | **Business Interests** leads the left column, directly under the Profile |
| Practice areas had their own headline block | Folded into a compact **Legal Practice** credential block at the foot of the left column |
| Right column held Practice + Business | Right column is now personal detail: Born, Family, Education, Contact, Location |

"Madras High Court" no longer appears anywhere in the document (verified against the rendered PDF).
The Panel Advocate credential, practice areas and enrollment number are retained, de-emphasised
to a single block, consistent with the client's note that the legal side should stay light.
